Paste from Jasper / Excel

Lines like IM 18357744 297083 290 (head code or short name, then one amount per office) fill the grid. A single column of 15 numbers pastes in Jasper order (IM, EX, CSF, CSF_EX, ARF, ECS, RCF, RDF, IFT, AIT, HHT, IDP, VAT, LUF, GT) into the office chosen below. CVF is added to IM.

Column mapping (which revenue codes feed each column)

Each column of the report adds up the revenue codes listed here (separate several codes with commas). The defaults follow your Maskebari Excel sheet; change them only if a column should include other heads.

Targets (Rs thousand) and month lengths

Office and sub-offices

Fiscal years (आर्थिक वर्ष)

Each fiscal year runs Shrawan to Ashadh and has its own targets and month lengths (set in the Targets tab). A year is also created automatically when you save or import data dated in it.

Revenue codes and heads

Add, rename or reorder revenue heads. The order here is the order in the entry grid and reports. Changing a code does not move amounts already saved under the old code.

Import from Excel

Choose your workbook. The sheet 108_Entry (Date, RevHead, name, office columns) is read; existing dates are overwritten. A Deposit sheet is read too if present.

Offices (कार्यालय)

Every office has its own separate data, targets, fiscal years and sub-offices. Deactivating an office blocks its users without deleting anything.

Users (प्रयोगकर्ता)

Each user belongs to exactly one office and sees only that office. To transfer a user, change their office and press Save; they see the new office after their next sign-in or Sync now. Read-only users can view and print but not change data.

Add user

“Create login and assign” makes a new sign-in (password at least 6 characters). If your Supabase project has sign-ups switched off, create the login in Supabase → Authentication → Users and then use “Assign existing login”.
